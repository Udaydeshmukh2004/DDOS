# IntelliShield SDN: Comparative Feature & Performance Analysis

This comparative evaluation benchmarks **Existing Systems** (traditional OpenFlow firewalls, static threshold mechanisms, and baseline hybrid systems such as Yadav et al. 2021) against the **Proposed System (IntelliShield SDN: A Self-Adaptive Hybrid Framework for DDoS Detection in Software-Defined Networks)** across 15 core architectural, operational, and algorithmic dimensions.

## 🎯 Key Qualitative Takeaways

1. **Detection & Accuracy:** While existing systems struggle with novel zero-day attacks and high false positive rates during flash crowds, **IntelliShield SDN** combines lightweight entropy math with supervised ensemble learning and deep autoencoders to achieve high detection accuracy (>99.3%) and sub-0.5% false positives.
2. **Mitigation Precision:** Rather than shutting down physical ports or whole IP subnets, **IntelliShield SDN** introduces surgical 5-tuple OpenFlow rules and automatic TCAM lifecycle eviction (`idle_timeout`/`hard_timeout`), ensuring benign traffic flows uninterrupted.
3. **Autonomy & Operations:** Through Explainable AI (XAI), closed-loop self-healing, and shadow-mode AutoML model promotion, **IntelliShield SDN** transitions software-defined network security from reactive manual intervention to proactive, autonomous defense.
